# Zoho Products and Services Documentation

## Zoho CRM - Customer Relationship Management

### Overview
Zoho CRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management software that helps businesses manage customer interactions, sales pipelines, and customer data efficiently. It streamlines customer management across multiple channels including email, social media, phone, and web.

### Core Features
- Contact and Account Management: Maintain comprehensive customer profiles with all communication history
- Sales Pipeline Management: Visualize deals at different stages and track sales progress
- Lead Management: Capture, qualify, and convert leads into customers
- Email Integration: Send emails directly from the CRM with automatic tracking and logging
- Task and Activity Management: Schedule follow-ups, calls, and meetings within the CRM
- Mobile App: Access CRM data on iOS and Android devices while on the go
- Custom Fields: Create custom fields to capture business-specific information
- Workflow Automation: Automate repetitive tasks with rules and triggers

### Common Issues and Resolution
- Login Issues: Clear browser cache and cookies, reset password if needed, check internet connection
- Data Synchronization: Ensure all users have completed the sync, check server status
- Email Not Syncing: Verify email account credentials, check bulk email limits (10,000 per day)
- Report Errors: Ensure you have proper permissions, verify data is not older than 2 years
- Import Failures: Check CSV file format, verify field mapping, ensure no duplicate entries in primary key field

### Pricing Plans
- Free Plan: Up to 3 users, basic CRM features
- Standard Plan: $18 per user/month, includes advanced features
- Professional Plan: $35 per user/month, includes custom automation
- Enterprise Plan: $50 per user/month, includes advanced customization

## Zoho Desk - Customer Service Software

### Overview
Zoho Desk is a cloud-based help desk software designed to provide customer support across multiple channels. It helps support teams manage tickets, track customer issues, and maintain knowledge bases.

### Key Features
- Multi-channel Support: Email, chat, phone, social media integration
- Ticket Management: Track, prioritize, and resolve customer support tickets
- Knowledge Base: Create self-service articles for customers
- Chat Widget: Add live chat widget to your website
- Automation Rules: Auto-assign tickets, send notifications, escalate priority issues
- Customer Community: Allow customers to help each other
- SLA Management: Set response and resolution time targets
- Performance Reports: Track team productivity and customer satisfaction

### Ticket Workflow
1. Customer submits ticket via email, chat, or web form
2. Ticket is automatically created and assigned to an agent
3. Agent reviews ticket and provides solution
4. Customer receives automated acknowledgment
5. Issue resolution and ticket closure
6. Customer satisfaction survey sent

### Integration Options
- CRM Integration: Link tickets to CRM contacts and accounts
- Email Sync: Automatically create tickets from emails
- Chat Widget: Embed on website for live customer interaction
- Social Media: Monitor and respond to social media inquiries
- Telephony: Call recording and logging with tickets

## Zoho Books - Accounting Software

### Overview
Zoho Books is cloud-based accounting software for small and medium-sized businesses. It simplifies invoicing, expense tracking, financial reporting, and accounting processes.

### Main Functionalities
- Invoicing: Create and send professional invoices with automatic reminders
- Expense Tracking: Track business expenses and create expense reports
- Bank Reconciliation: Automatically reconcile bank transactions
- Financial Reports: Generate profit & loss statements, balance sheets, cash flow reports
- Estimate Management: Create and track customer estimates
- Bill Payment: Pay bills and vendors directly from the system
- Tax Compliance: Track GST, VAT, and other tax information
- Multi-currency Support: Handle transactions in different currencies

### Invoice Management
- Create invoices with line items and taxes
- Set payment terms (Net 30, Net 60, etc.)
- Send reminders for overdue invoices
- Accept online payments (credit card, bank transfer)
- Track invoice status (draft, sent, paid, overdue)
- Bulk invoice creation for recurring customers

## Zoho Mail - Business Email

### Overview
Zoho Mail is a secure, ad-free email solution for businesses. It provides reliable email hosting with features like email forwarding, calendar, and contacts.

### Features
- Ad-free Email: No advertisements cluttering your inbox
- Mail Archive: Automatically archive emails after specified period
- Calendar Integration: Schedule meetings and manage events
- Contacts: Centralized contact management
- Email Forwarding: Forward emails from multiple accounts
- Spam Filter: Advanced spam detection and filtering
- Backup: Automatic email backup and recovery
- IMAP/POP Support: Use with any email client

### Email Limits
- Storage: 25GB per mailbox
- Maximum attachment size: 25MB
- Daily sending limit: 5,000 emails for new accounts (increased after verification)
